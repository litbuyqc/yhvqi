Oopbuy outfit planner

Start with what
you want to wear.

An outfit-based list helps you see whether a new find adds something useful or repeats what you already own.

01

Name the occasion

Write a specific use: a light travel outfit, a layered weekend look or a casual sports combination. Add climate and comfort requirements so your research has a clear direction.

02

Choose one anchor

Begin with the piece that sets the silhouette: shoes, trousers or a jacket. In your Oopbuy clothing guide notes, record what the remaining pieces need to match in length, volume and color.

03

Find the missing connection

Before collecting more Oopbuy fashion finds, identify the actual gap. You may need a plain T-shirt to balance a patterned layer, or a different trouser opening to suit a larger sole.

04

Keep a reason to revisit

Label each entry “compare,” “waiting for measurements” or “ready to review.” Save the date and unanswered question so an old link does not look like a recently checked item.

Turn a saved item into a useful record.

Keep the product URL, seller name, selected size and color together. Add measurements, listed materials, price currency and the date checked. Separate what the seller states from what you still need to confirm.

Illustrative entry / Not a live listing

Lightweight overshirt for an evening layer

Role: worn over an existing T-shirt. Fit target: enough chest room for layering, with sleeves that do not cover the hands. Open question: does the listed garment width refer to a flat measurement? Status: waiting for clarification.

  • ShoesRecord the model and size system. Compare model-specific fit notes and any supplied foot-length guidance; EU, US and UK labels are not interchangeable.
  • Tops & jerseysCompare chest width, body length, shoulder width and sleeve length with a similar garment you already wear. Note whether the intended fit is close, regular or oversized.
  • Pants & shortsCheck waist method, rise, inseam and leg opening. If a waist is elastic, distinguish relaxed and stretched measurements where supplied.
  • Sets & layersCheck each component separately. A matching label does not mean the top and bottom will both suit your proportions.
  • Headwear & jewelryRecord head or wrist dimensions, adjustment range, closure and listed materials. Ask for clarification where size information is missing.
  • UnderwearCheck the size chart, composition and hygiene-related return terms before ordering. Confirm policies with the seller rather than assuming returns are available.

International cost note

Compare the complete estimate.

Item price + domestic delivery + service charges + international shipping + applicable taxes or duties + currency conversion costs. Verify which charges are already included, keep the destination and currency visible, and mark unknown amounts as estimates.

Photos can help you inspect visible proportions, stitching and color differences, but lighting and camera angle affect appearance. They cannot establish authenticity, exact composition or how an item will feel when worn.

Can I download a spreadsheet from this page?

No downloadable spreadsheet is hosted here. You can recreate the suggested fields in your preferred spreadsheet application. Shopping links on this page lead to CAFANS.

How do I compare clothing sizes?

Compare the seller’s measurements for the exact variant with a similar garment that fits you. Use the same units and measurement method. A flat garment width is different from a body circumference.

Do brand photographs verify CAFANS products?

No. The photographs are visual references and do not establish CAFANS inventory, brand authorization or product authenticity. Check the exact seller listing and relevant documentation separately.

What should an international shopping budget include?

Record the item price, domestic delivery, service charges, international shipping, applicable taxes or duties, and currency conversion costs. Verify which charges are already included to avoid double counting.
